SEO FAQ

Frequently asked questions about SSI and digital credentials

Section optimized for organic search across informational and commercial intent.

What is an SSI platform?

An SSI (Self-Sovereign Identity) platform enables verifiable digital credentials, with user-controlled data and service-side cryptographic verification.

What is the difference between OID4VCI and OID4VP?

OID4VCI handles issuance of digital credentials to the wallet. OID4VP handles credential presentation and verification during login, onboarding, or authorization.

Why use it in an enterprise context?

It reduces fraud and user friction, improves auditability, and accelerates delivery of high-trust digital services in fintech, healthtech, HR, and regulated sectors.
